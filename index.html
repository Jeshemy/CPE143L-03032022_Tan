About Mapúa MCM

Established in 2015, Mapúa MCM is a branch that brings Mapúa University's legacy of technological and academic excellence to Mindanao. It officially began operations on July 2, 2018, with its formal inauguration on July 7, 2018.

Its classes follow Mapúa MCM FLUX, a hybrid flexible format that offers in-person, synchronous, or asynchronous online learning.

Vision: Mapúa Malayan Colleges Mindanao stands among the world's leading educational institutions.

Mission: To give students a learning environment that makes them globally competitive and locally in-demand, to carry out relevant research, development, and innovation, and to provide state-of-the-art solutions to industries and communities.
